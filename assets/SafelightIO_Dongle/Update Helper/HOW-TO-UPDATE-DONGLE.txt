SAFELIGHTIO DONGLE - FIRMWARE UPDATE
====================================

This guide updates the SafelightIO dongle from a Windows PC or a Mac.
You do not need to install any developer software.

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- The SafelightIO dongle.
- A USB-C data cable. A charge-only cable will not work.
- The new firmware file. It ends in .uf2.
- The BOOTSEL helper for your computer:
    Windows: bootsel-windows.bat
    Mac:     bootsel-mac.command

On Windows, keep bootsel-windows.ps1 in the same folder as
bootsel-windows.bat. Do not rename the helper files.

BEFORE YOU START
----------------
1. Close the SafelightIO app on your phone.
2. Close any program that may be using the dongle's serial port.
3. Keep the dongle, the cable and the computer still while updating.


WINDOWS - STEP BY STEP
----------------------
1. Put bootsel-windows.bat, bootsel-windows.ps1 and the .uf2 file
   in the same folder.

2. Plug the dongle into the PC with the data cable.

3. Double-click bootsel-windows.bat.

4. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC":
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   Administrator rights are not needed.

5. A black window opens. Wait while it looks for the dongle and
   asks it to enter update mode.

6. If the window asks you to choose a COM port, type the number for
   the SafelightIO dongle and press Enter.

7. When the window says the dongle is in update mode, open File
   Explorer. A new drive called RP2350 should appear.

8. Copy the .uf2 file onto the RP2350 drive.

9. Wait until the RP2350 drive disappears. This can take a few
   seconds. Do NOT unplug the dongle while the drive is visible.

10. Unplug the dongle and plug it back into your phone.
    Open the SafelightIO app. The top bar should say
    "Ready - fw ...".


MAC - STEP BY STEP
------------------
1. Put bootsel-mac.command and the .uf2 file in the same folder.

2. Plug the dongle into the Mac with the data cable.

3. Double-click bootsel-mac.command.

4. If macOS will not open it, right-click the file, choose "Open",
   then click "Open" again.

5. A Terminal window opens. Wait while it looks for the dongle and
   asks it to enter update mode.

6. When it says the dongle is in update mode, open Finder.
   A new drive called RP2350 should appear under Locations.

7. Drag the .uf2 file onto the RP2350 drive.

8. Wait until the RP2350 drive disappears. Do NOT unplug the dongle
   while the drive is visible.

9. Unplug the dongle and plug it back into your phone.
   Open the SafelightIO app. The top bar should say
   "Ready - fw ...".


IF IT DOES NOT WORK
-------------------
"No SafelightIO Dongle USB device was found"
- Use a data cable, not a charge-only cable.
- Close the SafelightIO app and any serial program.
- Unplug the dongle and plug it in again.
- Try another USB port on the computer.

"Could not open the port at 1200 baud"
- Another program is using the dongle. Close it and try again.

The RP2350 drive does not appear
- Wait 20 seconds, then run the helper again.
- The helper only works while the normal firmware is running.
- If the dongle is already in update mode, the RP2350 drive should
  already be visible; just copy the .uf2 file.

The update was interrupted
- Do not repeatedly unplug the dongle.
- If it no longer starts, the sealed unit may need service recovery.
  Contact SafelightIO support.

CHECK THAT IT WORKED
--------------------
Open the SafelightIO app and connect the dongle.
The top bar should say "Ready - fw ..." with the new version.
You can also open Setup > Meter to see the firmware version and the
SafelightIO ID.

Your paper calibrations, settings and catalogue are kept. Updating
the firmware does not erase them.

NEED HELP?
----------
Send the exact message shown in the black window or Terminal, and say
whether you are on Windows or Mac.
